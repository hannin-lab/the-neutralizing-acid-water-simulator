Why does this matter?

Water with pH below 6.5 corrodes metal pipes, leaches heavy metals, and harms aquatic ecosystems. Over 2 billion people worldwide lack access to safe water (WHO, 2023). Understanding which affordable household substances can raise pH toward the safe range is a practical, real-world problem — especially in communities without industrial water treatment.

🏠

If water at home feels acidic — here's what you can actually do

If you notice signs of acidic water (metallic taste, corroded taps, pH below 6.5), baking soda or lime paste can raise pH for non-drinking purposes. These are validated, low-cost options for:

Watering acid-sensitive plants — neutralized water helps plants that prefer neutral soil
Cleaning metal surfaces — less acidic water causes less corrosion on pipes and cookware
Industrial/agricultural use — neutralizing runoff before it enters drainage systems
For safe drinking water — always use a certified water filter or contact local health authorities
